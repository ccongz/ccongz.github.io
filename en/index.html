Biography

Dr. Cong Zhang is an Associate Professor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, SMBU. He received his Ph.D. in Computing Science from Simon Fraser University in 2018 under the supervision of Prof. Jiangchuan Liu.

His research spans multimedia networking and systems, cloud-edge collaborative computing, and foundation models for energy and power systems. He has published more than 50 papers in venues including ACM MobiCom, ACM UbiComp/IMWUT, IEEE INFOCOM, ACM Multimedia, IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, and IEEE Internet of Things Journal. He has led or contributed to projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the National Key R&D Program of China, and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.

Recruiting

I am looking for self-motivated master's and doctoral students in Computer Science, as well as visiting graduate students for research stays of at least six months. Current topics include foundation models for energy systems, intelligent satellite networking systems, and cloud-edge intelligence.

Prospective students are welcome to email me with a brief introduction, CV, transcript, and representative research or project experience. Please use the subject [Application] Name – University – Research Interest.

Research Interests

01

AI for Energy and Power Systems

Developing robust foundation models, multimodal learning methods, and time-series forecasting systems for grid inspection, load forecasting, virtual power plants, and low-carbon energy management.

02

Multimedia and Satellite Networking Systems

Improving interactive video, live streaming, 360-degree media, and emerging satellite networking systems through learning-assisted networking, intelligent resource management, and resilient transmission.

03

Cloud-Edge Collaborative Intelligence

Designing efficient resource coordination, distributed inference, edge learning, and deployable AI infrastructure for latency-sensitive industrial applications.
